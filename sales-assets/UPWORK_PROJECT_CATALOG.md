## Project summary

Turn completed jobs into honest customer feedback and more opportunities for public reviews with a focused, white-label system built for your business.

I will configure ReviewDesk with your branding, approved email copy, official review link, and customer workflow. It sends review requests and reminders, collects every rating privately, stops reminders after a response, and gives you a simple dashboard for follow-up.

Unlike review-gating funnels, the public review option is presented to every genuine customer regardless of rating. No fake reviews, incentives, or rating manipulation are included.

Depending on your package, you receive:

- branded mobile feedback page;
- three-step email request sequence;
- customer dashboard and response analytics;
- CSV import and export;
- private feedback library;
- permission-based testimonial wall and website widget;
- SMTP delivery setup;
- one completed-job webhook or automation connection;
- deployment and operating documentation.

## FAQ

### Does this create or buy reviews?

No. It requests honest feedback only from genuine customers. It does not fabricate reviews, offer incentives, or influence the rating.

### Does it hide the public review link from unhappy customers?

No. The public review option is presented consistently regardless of the private rating.

### Are monthly software fees required?

ReviewDesk is self-hosted. Hosting, email-provider, and optional automation-provider costs remain the buyer's responsibility.

### Can it work with my CRM?

The Advanced package includes one compatible webhook or automation connection. Message me with the CRM name before ordering if it uses a proprietary API.

### Can agencies resell it?

Yes, with a separately agreed white-label or multi-client license and one deployment per client.
