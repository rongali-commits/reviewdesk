## Description

Good service does not automatically become social proof. Most satisfied customers simply move on, while owners forget to ask at the right moment.

I will customize and deploy ReviewDesk, a white-label system that sends approved review requests, collects honest private feedback, tracks responses, and turns permissioned customer stories into a website widget.

You receive a real working product, not a mockup:

- branded mobile feedback page;
- automated email request and reminders;
- customer and feedback dashboard;
- neutral public review handoff for every rating;
- CSV import and safe export;
- optional SMTP setup;
- permission-based testimonial wall;
- optional webhook or automation connection;
- deployment and documentation.

This service does not sell fake reviews, incentivize customers, or filter public-review access based on rating.

## FAQ

### Will I receive fake or purchased reviews?

No. ReviewDesk supports honest requests to genuine customers only.

### Will low ratings be blocked from the public review option?

No. Every customer receives the same public-review option.

### Do I need a monthly ReviewDesk subscription?

No. It is self-hosted. Your hosting and email provider may charge their own fees.
