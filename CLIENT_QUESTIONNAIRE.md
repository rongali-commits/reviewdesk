# ReviewDesk client questionnaire

Send this after the order starts. Do not begin production email delivery until the buyer approves the final answers.

## Business identity

1. Legal or public business name
2. Short brand name
3. Logo file or one to three logo letters
4. Primary and accent hex colors
5. Tagline
6. Public contact email
7. Privacy-policy URL

## Review destination

1. Official Google, Trustpilot, Facebook, Yelp, or industry review-page URL
2. Preferred button label
3. Confirmation that the destination belongs to the buyer's business
4. Confirmation that requests will be sent only to genuine customers

## Request delivery

1. Approved sender name
2. Verified reply-to email
3. Approved SMTP provider
4. Preferred initial-request timing
5. Preferred reminder timing
6. Final approved subject lines and message copy

## Customer data

1. CSV headers or webhook source
2. Typical service or purchase description
3. Customer consent basis for email requests
4. Required data-retention period
5. Person responsible for handling private feedback

## Testimonials

1. Should the testimonial wall be enabled?
2. Where should the widget be embedded?
3. Who approves permissioned stories before publication?
4. Should customer surnames be shortened for display?

## Launch approval

The buyer must approve the business identity, message sequence, public review link, customer-data source, privacy copy, and test results in writing before production activation.
