# ReviewDesk fulfillment playbook

## Starter: branded review-request system

Target delivery: 2 days.

1. Collect the questionnaire and brand assets.
2. Configure the business identity and official review link.
3. Customize the customer feedback page.
4. Configure three approved email messages in demo mode.
5. Deploy one private business instance.
6. Test a sample customer request and feedback submission.
7. Deliver the dashboard token, request link workflow, and operating guide.

## Standard: automation and testimonial widget

Target delivery: 4 days.

Includes Starter, plus:

1. Configure CSV import and the customer's approved data columns.
2. Connect a verified SMTP provider.
3. Customize message timing and templates.
4. Configure testimonial consent and the embeddable widget.
5. Run inbox-delivery and suppression tests.
6. Provide two revision rounds.

## Advanced: connected reputation workflow

Target delivery: 6 days.

Includes Standard, plus:

1. Connect one supported webhook or automation source.
2. Customize the completed-service trigger.
3. Add the widget to one buyer-controlled website.
4. Configure persistent production storage and backups.
5. Run end-to-end acceptance tests with buyer-approved records.
6. Provide three revision rounds and a recorded handoff.

## Scope controls

- One business and one deployment per order unless a package says otherwise.
- SMS, WhatsApp, proprietary CRM integrations, multi-location tenancy, and custom authentication require a separate quote.
- The buyer supplies lawful customer data, a verified sender, and the official review URL.
- No fake, paid, incentivized, or selectively solicited reviews are included.
- Platform compliance and legal review remain the buyer's responsibility.
